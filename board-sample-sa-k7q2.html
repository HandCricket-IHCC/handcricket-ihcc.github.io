About this board

South Africa is a member board of the International Hand Cricket Council. Its players take part in IHCC tournaments, series and elections under the IHCC Constitution.

Board election. Held on 27 October 2026. The board's election was reported to IHCC, and IHCC keeps the record.

    Captain and Vice Captain

    The Team Director or the Deputy Director can choose them, alone, at any time. They must be among the first 12 players (the voting members). The Captain then comes first in the list and the Vice Captain second.

    Preview

    Move a player into the first 12

    The first 12 players are the voting members. To bring a player from 13 to 20 into the first 12, he swaps places with one of the first 12. The Team Director or the Deputy Director starts the request. Then the 12 voting members sign in and approve or reject. 7 approvals (more than half) make the swap happen at once. 7 rejections end it. If neither is reached in 48 hours, the request ends by itself and nothing changes. This cannot be done from the day the Voting List closes until the result of the election.

    The two players get one email now, and one when the decision is made. The player moving out can write his answer on the page, and the 12 voting members will see it.

    Approvals: 0 of 7

    Rejections: 0 of 7

    Time left: 48 hours
