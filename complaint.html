Sign in

Only a member (a player in a squad) or an officer of IHCC can make a complaint (Constitution 25.1). Sign in with the Google account that is in your squad. A complaint without a name is not accepted. If you are afraid, you can ask that your name is not shown to the accused. The accused is always told what is said against him, so that he can answer.

If you are outside IHCC, send the information by email to the General Secretary.

New complaint

Complaints open on 3 December 2026.

Time limit: ninety days from the act or from the day it became known. For serious offences (match fixing, a false vote or buying votes, misuse of IHCC money, threats and pressure) one year. A complaint about an election goes to the Board of Directors under Constitution 16.16.
