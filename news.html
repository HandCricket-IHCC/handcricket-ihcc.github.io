News and announcements

Official announcements of the International Hand Cricket Council.

Latest announcements

Every Member Board now has its own page

1 November 2026

The 13 Member Boards each have a page with the board's leaders, its squad and its announcements. Only the display name and the real name of a player are shown, and nothing else about a player is ever public. The Team Director, the Deputy Director and the Media Manager of a board look after the page from a Management page.

Open the Member Boards

ODI is now 16 overs

31 October 2026

The Boards changed the length of ODI from 15 to 16 overs. All 13 Member Boards approved it, which is more than the two-thirds majority needed. T20 stays 12 overs and Test stays 20 overs.

IHCC is now on YouTube

26 October 2026

The official IHCC YouTube channel is now open. Its name is IHCC Hand Cricket and its handle is @IHCCHandCricket.

Open the YouTube channel

Match formats decided

25 October 2026

The Boards decided the length of the three formats: T20 is 12 overs, ODI is 15 overs and Test is 20 overs.

Update of 31 October 2026: ODI is now 16 overs. See the news of that day.

Decision on player display names

24 October 2026

The Boards voted on the display names of players. Of the 15 Boards, 11 voted: 9 in favour and 2 against. Every player, male or female, uses the verified name of a male cricketer of his or her board's country. The cricketer must have played at least one international match for that country, in T20, ODI or Test. A cricketer who has played only domestic cricket cannot be used.

Squad lists now go through board representatives

24 October 2026

Each Member Board now sends its squad list through one board representative, who signs in with Google. Players do not fill in a form themselves. A board needs 12 to 20 players, and the first 12 are the voters. The last date is 31 October 2026, 11:59 PM Pakistan time.
