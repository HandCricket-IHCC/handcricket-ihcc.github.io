Elections

The IHCC elections: dates, who can vote, how nominations work, and the buttons for Nominations and Voting.

Go to

Nominations   Voting

Nominations are for Team Directors. Voting is for the voters of the 13 Member Boards. Both pages ask you to sign in with Google, using the Gmail address that is in the squad list.

What is elected

Three seats: the Chairman, the Deputy Chairman and the General Secretary.

  1. The voters are the first 12 players of each of the 13 Member Boards. That is 156 voters.
  2. Each voter has one vote for each seat. A vote is final.
  3. The result pages show only the turnout and the totals. They never show who voted for whom.

Practice election

The practice election was held on 3 and 4 November 2026 with the same steps. It is finished, its result did not count, and its data has been deleted.

Real election

  1. Nominations: 8 November to 10 November 2026. They close at 11:59 PM Pakistan time on 10 November.
  2. Campaign: until 14 November 2026.
  3. Voting: 15 November 2026, from 6:00 AM to 6:00 PM, Pakistan time. If not more than half of the voters take part, the voting is repeated on 22 November 2026.

Voting opens and closes by itself at the times above.

Voting turnout

Loading the numbers.

Only numbers are shown, never names and never who voted for whom.

How nominations work

  1. The Team Director of a board starts the nomination. The nominee must be from the same board.
  2. The nominee agrees. A proposer from the nominee's board and a seconder from another board confirm.
  3. Each board can nominate one person for a seat.

Add the dates to your calendar

  • Nominations, 8 to 10 November 2026 (calendar file)
  • Voting, 15 November 2026, 6:00 AM to 6:00 PM Pakistan time (calendar file)

Open the file and your calendar app adds the date with a reminder one day before. The second voting day, 22 November, is only used if not more than half of the voters take part.
