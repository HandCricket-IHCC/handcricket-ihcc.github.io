    About this board

    Announcements

    Leadership

    The board chose them in its own election, under the IHCC Constitution. A WhatsApp button appears only when the person has allowed it.

    Team

    The whole squad in the order chosen by the board. The first 12 are the voting members. Only the display name and the real name are shown. Nothing else about a player is ever shown on this site.

    New players join in two steps: the Team Director of the board approves first, then the IHCC General Secretary. Removing and swapping players is done by the board's leaders, from their Management page, after the elections are finished.

    Join this board

    To join, sign in with the Gmail address you use for Hand Cricket. This proves the address is yours.
