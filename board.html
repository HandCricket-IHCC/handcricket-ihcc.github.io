    About this board

    Announcements

    Leadership

    The board chose them in its own election, under the IHCC Constitution. A WhatsApp button appears only when the person has allowed it.

    Team

    The whole squad in the order chosen by the board. The first 12 are the voting members. Only the display name and the real name are shown. Nothing else about a player is ever shown on this site.

    Joining is closed for now. Removing and swapping players is done by the board's leaders, from their Management page, after the elections are finished.

      Report a problem

      Is a name wrong, or is something on this page not suitable? Tell IHCC. The button opens an email to the IHCC official address, with the board's name and this page's link already written in it.
