    About this board

    Announcements

    Leadership

    The board chose them in its own election, under the IHCC Constitution. A WhatsApp button appears only when the person has allowed it.

    Team

    The whole squad in the order chosen by the board. The first 12 are the voting members. Only the display name and the real name are shown. Nothing else about a player is ever shown on this site.

    Joining, removal and swapping of players are closed for now. They will be set up by IHCC after the elections are finished.

      Report a problem

      Is a name wrong, or is something on this page not suitable? Tell IHCC. The button opens an email to the IHCC official address, with the board's name and this page's link already written in it.

      Report a problem by email

      The email goes to handcricket.ihcc@gmail.com

      Board leaders

      Are you the Team Director, the Deputy Director or the Media Manager of this board? Sign in with the Google account (Gmail) you gave in your squad form. The Manage board part then opens for you. Nobody else can open it; the server checks it.

      Manage board

      New since your last visit

        Put an announcement live

        It goes live on the board page at once. The board can put 3 announcements live per day (Pakistan time). If you delete one within 15 minutes, that place is given back. The other two leaders see it in their notifications. Up to 1000 characters.

        0 of 1000 characters

        Live announcements

          Captain and Vice Captain

          Only the Team Director or the Deputy Director can choose. Both must be among the first 12 players. The Captain moves to number 1 and the Vice Captain to number 2; the others move down.

          Board logo

          Only the Team Director can change it. PNG or JPG, under 1 MB. It goes live at once and IHCC gets a report. The logo can be changed 3 times per day.

          My WhatsApp contact

          Each leader looks after only his own number. When the button is off, the page says "WhatsApp: Not available". Only you can switch it on. If you do not switch it on, nobody sees your number.

          With the country code, for example +92 300 1234567.

          About this board

          All three leaders can write it. Only one About text is kept: a new one replaces the old one. Up to 250 characters. Every change is written in the Activity list below.
