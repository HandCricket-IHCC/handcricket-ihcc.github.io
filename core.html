Core Committee

The six offices of the IHCC Core Committee, who holds them, and what each office does.

The six offices

The Core Committee runs the daily work of IHCC (Constitution Article 14). The Chairman, the Deputy Chairman and the General Secretary are elected by the Voting Members. The CEO, the CFO and the Women's Director are elected by the Board of Directors. The first terms begin on 3 December 2026 and end on 2 December 2027.

Loading the offices.

How to reach the Core Committee

  1. A complaint is made in writing to the General Secretary, by email to the official IHCC address or in the Main Group. It says who, what and when, and gives the proof (Rule Book rule 8.1).
  2. A member who does not agree with a decision of the General Secretary may ask the Core Committee in writing, within seven days, to review it (Constitution clause 14.12A).
  3. The official IHCC email address is handcricket.ihcc@gmail.com.
  4. Gmail addresses and WhatsApp numbers of the officers are not published.

Sign in for officers

Officers sign in with Google, with the Gmail address that IHCC knows. Sign-in for the Core Committee opens on 3 December 2026, when the Constitution comes into force.
