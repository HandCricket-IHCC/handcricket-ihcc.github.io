Complaints against the General Secretary or the Chairman

Constitution 25.2 and 25.2A: you take and run these complaints. Nobody else can see them, not even the owner of the website. Acknowledge in writing within twenty-four hours and tell the accused. The accused has three days to answer. Then the investigating officer has fourteen days.

General Secretary not answering

Constitution 14.18: if the General Secretary has not answered official matters for fourteen days and gave no leave notice, you give him a written notice by email and WhatsApp. He has seven days to answer. If he does not, you declare the office vacant in writing and the Directors confirm it within seven days by more than half, or it ends. This is not a sanction.

Leave of the Chairman

Constitution 14.18: the Chairman may take written leave of up to thirty days, once a year. You approve it.
