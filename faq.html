International Hand Cricket Council

Questions and answers

Answers to the most common questions about IHCC, its Member Boards and the squad lists.

Frequently asked questions

What is IHCC?

The International Hand Cricket Council is the governing body being formed by the Hand Cricket community. It works under one written constitution, with open elections and equal Member Boards.

Roman Urdu: IHCC yaani International Hand Cricket Council woh idara hai jo Hand Cricket ki community bana rahi hai. Yeh ek likhe hue aaeen (constitution) ke tehat chalta hai, jis mein khuli elections aur barabar ke Member Boards hain.

How many Member Boards are there?

There are 13 Member Boards. Each one has its own page, with its leaders, its squad and its announcements. Open the Boards page to see them.

Roman Urdu: 13 Member Boards hain. Har board ka apna page hai, jis mein uske leaders, squad aur announcements hain. Dekhne ke liye Boards page kholen.

Who looks after a board's page?

The Team Director, the Deputy Director and the Media Manager of the board. They sign in with their Gmail through Leader login on the board page. The sign-in opens once the board has reported its own election to IHCC and IHCC has recorded its leaders.

Roman Urdu: Board ke Team Director, Deputy Director aur Media Manager. Woh apni Gmail se board page par Leader login ke zariye sign in karte hain. Sign-in tab khulta hai jab board apni election IHCC ko report kar de aur IHCC uske leaders record kar le.

Can I still send a squad list?

No. Squad submission closed on 31 October 2026, 11:59 PM Pakistan time. Each board had sent its list through one board representative. To correct a detail now, write to IHCC at handcricket.ihcc@gmail.com.

Roman Urdu: Nahi. Squad bhejne ki aakhri tareekh 31 October 2026, raat 11:59 Pakistan time thi, jo guzar chuki hai. Har board ne apni list ek board representative ke zariye bheji thi. Ab koi tafseel theek karwani ho to handcricket.ihcc@gmail.com par likhein.

How many players must a board have?

A board needs at least 12 players and can have up to 20.

Roman Urdu: Board ke kam az kam 12 players hone chahiye, aur zyada se zyada 20 ho sakte hain.

Which players can vote?

The first 12 players of a board's squad are its voting members. The board decides the order of its squad.

Roman Urdu: Board ke squad ke pehle 12 players voting members hain. Squad ki tarteeb board khud tay karta hai.

What can a player's display name be?

The verified name of a real cricketer of the board's country, who has played at least one international match for that country in T20 (Twenty20), ODI (One Day International) or Test. A male player uses the name of a male cricketer. A female player uses the name of a female cricketer or, if she wishes, of a male cricketer. A cricketer who has played only domestic cricket cannot be used.

Roman Urdu: Display name us mulk ke asli cricketer ka tasdeeq shuda naam hona chahiye jis ne us mulk ke liye kam az kam ek international match T20, ODI ya Test mein khela ho. Mard player mard cricketer ka naam istemal karta hai. Khatoon player khatoon cricketer ka naam istemal karti hai, ya chahe to mard cricketer ka. Jis cricketer ne sirf domestic cricket khela ho, uska naam istemal nahi ho sakta.

Can two players have the same real name?

Yes. Only the Gmail address, the WhatsApp number and the display name must be different for each player of a board.

Roman Urdu: Ji haan. Har player ka sirf Gmail address, WhatsApp number aur display name board mein alag alag hona zaroori hai.

How long are the three formats?

T20 is 12 overs, ODI is 16 overs and Test is 20 overs.

Roman Urdu: T20 12 over ka, ODI 16 over ka aur Test 20 over ka hai.

Does a player get a confirmation?

When a player was added, or his details changed, he got an email with his details and a registration number. If he does not see it, he should check the spam folder.

Roman Urdu: Jab kisi player ko add kiya gaya ya uski tafseel badli, to usay email mili jis mein uski tafseel aur registration number tha. Na mile to spam folder dekh lein.

What if a detail is wrong?

Write to IHCC at handcricket.ihcc@gmail.com with the registration number, and the entry will be corrected.

Roman Urdu: Registration number ke saath handcricket.ihcc@gmail.com par likhein, entry theek kar di jayegi.

Who can see my details?

The public sees only the display name and the real name of each player, on the page of the board. Your Gmail address, WhatsApp number, city and country are never public. A leader's WhatsApp button is shown only if that leader switches it on himself. IHCC management sees the rest, only as much as each person needs for the work.

Roman Urdu: Public ko sirf har player ka display name aur asli naam board ke page par nazar aata hai. Aap ka Gmail address, WhatsApp number, shehar aur mulk kabhi public nahi hote. Leader ka WhatsApp button sirf tab dikhta hai jab leader khud usay on kare. Baqi maloomat IHCC ki management dekhti hai, utni hi jitni har shakhs ko kaam ke liye chahiye.

Can I search for a player?

Yes. On the Boards page there is a search box. Type at least 3 letters of a display name or a real name. The result shows only the display name, the real name, the board and the place in the squad. Nothing else about a player is ever shown.

Roman Urdu: Ji haan. Boards page par ek search box hai. Display name ya asli naam ke kam az kam 3 huroof likhein. Nateeje mein sirf display name, asli naam, board aur squad mein jagah nazar aati hai. Player ki aur koi maloomat kabhi nahi dikhai jati.

Where are announcements made?

On the page of each board, on the News page and on the official IHCC WhatsApp channel.

Roman Urdu: Har board ke page par, News page par aur IHCC ke official WhatsApp channel par.

How do I contact IHCC?

Write to handcricket.ihcc@gmail.com, or follow the official WhatsApp channel.

Roman Urdu: handcricket.ihcc@gmail.com par likhein, ya official WhatsApp channel follow karein.
