How the squads were made

  1. Each board sent its squad list through one board representative. The representative signed in with Google and added the players one at a time.
  2. A board needs 12 to 20 players. The first 12 are the voting members.
  3. Now the squads are on the board pages. Each board page shows the display name and the real name of every player. The leaders of the board look after the page.

Where are the squads now?

Squad submission is closed. The squad of every Member Board is shown on its own board page, with the display name and the real name of each player. Nothing else about a player is public.

Questions

How many players must a board have?

A board needs at least 12 players and can have up to 20. A board with fewer than 12 players by the last date is not eligible.

Which players can vote?

The first 12 players of a board's squad are its voting members. The board decides the order of its squad.

What if my details are wrong, or I did not agree to be added?

Write to IHCC at handcricket.ihcc@gmail.com with your registration number.

Who can see the details?

The public sees only the display name and the real name of each player, on the page of the board. Your Gmail address, WhatsApp number, city and country are never public.
