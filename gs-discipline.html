Sanctions register

Constitution Article 25. Record each sanction after it is decided. A small sanction is told only to the person and the Core Committee. The record is kept two years for small, five for medium, always for large. The third time for the same offence moves to the next level.

Appeals

Constitution 25.9 and 25.9A. The appeal is in writing within seven days, on one ground. You check within twenty-four hours whether there is a ground. A medium sanction goes to five Directors drawn by lot, a large one to the full Board. The decision is within fourteen days and can never make the sanction heavier. Open an appeal from the sanction above.

Pardon

Constitution 25.16. Only the Board of Directors may forgive or reduce a sanction, and no single officer. At least half of the sanction must have passed without a new offence. A small sanction is not forgiven. A permanent ban may be asked about after two years, and needs nine of twelve Directors. The person asks in writing through you, with a reason, and the decision is within fourteen days. A general pardon for everyone is not allowed. The record stays.

No confidence in a Team Director or Deputy Director

Constitution 11.8B. At least eight of the twelve Voting Members say it in writing. Not in the first ninety days of the term, nor from the closing of the Voting List until the election result. The office ends, the board elects a new holder within thirty days and tells you. It is not a sanction.

Interim measures

Constitution 25.2B. The Chairman proposes it on his desk, and you approve it here. The Directors must confirm within seven days by more than half, or it ends by itself. It lasts at most thirty days and is not a sanction. The accused may object in writing within forty-eight hours.
