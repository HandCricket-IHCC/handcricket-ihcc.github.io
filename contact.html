Help and contact

How to reach IHCC, where announcements are posted, and which page answers which question.

Write to us

Email: handcricket.ihcc@gmail.com. Please write a clear subject, for example "Election", "Squad list" or "Accessibility problem". Never send a password.

Follow IHCC

  • WhatsApp channel: announcements and reminders.
  • YouTube channel: videos and live streams.
  • News page: the official announcements, the same as on the channel.

Which help do you need?

  • A mistake in a player name or in a squad list: write to the Team Director of your Board. The Team Director writes to us.
  • A problem with nominations or voting: open the Elections page first, then write to us with the subject "Election". Say which Gmail you signed in with.
  • The website does not work with your screen reader or phone: see the Accessibility page.
  • What data we keep: see the Privacy page.
  • Common questions: see the FAQ.
