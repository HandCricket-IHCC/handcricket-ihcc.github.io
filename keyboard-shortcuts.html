Keyboard shortcuts

Keys to move around the IHCC website without a mouse: public pages, board pages, the election pages and the admin page.

How to use them

Hold the Alt key, press the letter or number, then let go of both. Example: Alt + B opens the Boards page.

  1. The shortcuts are for a computer keyboard on Windows or Linux. They are made for Google Chrome. Other browsers may work but are not checked yet. They are not available on phones and tablets, or on a Mac for now.
  2. They use the physical key, so they work with any keyboard language.
  3. Every shortcut is only a faster way. The menu, links and buttons on the page always do the same thing.
  4. A shortcut does nothing while a message box is open. Close the box first, with Escape.
  5. We do not use Alt + F, E or D, Alt + Home or Alt + the arrow keys, because Chrome uses them.

Board pages and Management

These work on the page of a board and on its Management page, for the Team Director, the Deputy Director and the Media Manager.

Election and nomination pages

At the bottom of these pages there are links to Home, Boards, News and this page. Alt + H, B, N and K work there too.

If a shortcut does not work

  1. Your browser, Windows or a screen reader add-on may already use that key. Use the menu or the links instead.
  2. Check that you are holding only Alt, without Shift or Control.
  3. Tell us which key and which browser, at the email address in the footer, and we will change it.
