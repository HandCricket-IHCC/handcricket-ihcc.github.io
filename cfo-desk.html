Accounts

Constitution 14.14 and Article 22: you keep the accounts. Money stays in your account for now (Decision 8). Write every amount in Pakistani rupees.

Fines given in charity

Constitution 25.7: every fine is given in charity within thirty days, and the Chairman and you choose the charity together.

Badge prices owed

Constitution 9.8: as sponsorship money comes in, first pay the persons who have left or been removed and whose time has passed, in the order in which they left. The rest is paid gradually, giving every board an equal turn. Only the full price is paid, never a part. The list below is in that order.
