          Site notice

          A bar at the top of every page of the website, for urgent words such as a change in the voting time. It shows only while it is switched on and between the start and end times. Without times it shows until you switch it off. Every change is written in the audit log.

          Send email

          Each person gets his own email from International Hand Cricket Council. It is signed IHCC Management, the subject starts with [IHCC], and replies come to the IHCC Gmail. Campaigns may use 240 emails a day. If a group is bigger, the rest is sent automatically the next day.
