      Announcements

      Put an announcement live

      It goes live on the board page at once. The board can put 3 announcements live per day (Pakistan time). If you delete one within 15 minutes, that place is given back. The other two leaders see it in their notifications.

        Squad

        Joining, removing and swapping players

        These are closed until the elections are finished. IHCC will set them up after that. Joining is also closed on the public page.

        The squad now

          Captain and Vice Captain

          Only the Team Director or the Deputy Director can choose. Both must be among the first 12 players. The Captain moves to number 1 and the Vice Captain to number 2; the others move down.

          Logo and About

          About this board

          All three leaders can write it. Only one About text is kept: a new one replaces the old one. Up to 250 characters. Every change is written in the Activity list.

          0 of 250 characters

          Board logo

          Only the Team Director can change it. PNG or JPG, under 1 MB. It goes live at once and IHCC gets a report. The logo can be changed 3 times per day.

          My WhatsApp

          Each leader looks after only his own number. When the button is off, the page says "WhatsApp: Not available". Only you can switch it on. If you do not switch it on, nobody sees your number.

          With the country code, for example +92 300 1234567.
