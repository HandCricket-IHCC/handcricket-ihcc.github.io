Order a meeting of the Directors

Constitution 13.8: you order the meeting and the General Secretary calls it with twenty-four hours' notice. If you do not, at least five Directors may demand it in writing.

Leave of Core officers

Constitution 14.18: a Core officer may take written leave of up to thirty days, once a year. You approve it, except your own, which the Deputy Chairman approves.

An officer not answering

Constitution 14.18: if a Core officer has not answered official matters for fourteen days and gave no leave notice, the General Secretary gives him a written notice by email and WhatsApp, and he has seven days to answer. Record it here. If he does not answer, you declare the office vacant in writing, and the Directors confirm it within seven days by more than half, or it ends. This is not a sanction. For the General Secretary, the Deputy Chairman does this.
