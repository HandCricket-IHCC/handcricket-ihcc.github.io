International Hand Cricket Council

Privacy and data

What data IHCC records, why, who can see it and which services are used.

What is recorded

When you submit the squad registration form, IHCC records:

  1. The details you type: board, display name, real name, email address, WhatsApp number, city and country.
  2. Your three agreements: joining the board of your own free will, accepting the IHCC Constitution, and agreeing to these data terms.
  3. A registration number that is made for you.

Why

The details you type are used for squad registration and for communication with you.

Who can see it

Only IHCC administrators who sign in, and each sees only what their role needs. Board directors will see only the players of their own board. Every change an administrator makes is written in an audit log.

The public sees only one thing: how many players each board has registered, as a number. No name or detail is shown.

Keeping and correcting data

To correct your details, write to handcricket.ihcc@gmail.com with your registration number.

Agreement

The registration form asks you to agree to these terms before you can submit it. This page may be updated, and the date at the bottom shows when it was last changed.

Contact IHCC: handcricket.ihcc@gmail.com
