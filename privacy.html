Privacy and data

What data IHCC records, why, who can see it and which services are used.

What is recorded

When a board representative added you to a squad list, IHCC recorded:

  1. The details the representative typed: board, display name, real name, Gmail address, WhatsApp number, city and country.
  2. The representative's confirmation that he has permission from every player he adds, that the details are correct, and that he agrees to these data terms. IHCC also notes technical details such as the IP address and device of the representative, only to keep registration and elections fair.
  3. A registration number that is made for each player. The player got it by email.

On a board page, the leaders of the board can also put up announcements, a board logo, an About text, and choose a Captain and a Vice Captain. A leader can add his own WhatsApp number for the page. IHCC records these changes, who made them and when.

Why

The details are used for the squad lists, for voting by the voting members, for the board pages and for communication with the player.

Who can see it

The public sees only the display name and the real name of each player, on the page of the board, and who is the Team Director, Deputy Director, Media Manager, Captain and Vice Captain. Your Gmail address, WhatsApp number, city and country are never public.

A leader's WhatsApp button is shown on the board page only if that leader switches it on himself, with his own number. If he does not, the page says "WhatsApp: Not available".

Board leaders who sign in see only the management of their own board. IHCC administrators who sign in see only what their role needs. Every change an administrator or a leader makes is written in a log.

Sign in with Google

Board leaders and IHCC administrators sign in with their Google account. IHCC receives only the Gmail address, and the name and profile picture that Google shares with every sign-in. This is used only to check that the person is on the list of leaders or administrators, and to show who made a change.

IHCC does not read your emails, contacts, files or any other data in your Google account. The sign-in data is not sold and is not shared with anyone outside IHCC management. It is stored with the website's database provider. You can sign out at any time, and you can ask for your sign-in record to be removed by writing to handcricket.ihcc@gmail.com.

Keeping and correcting data

If any detail is wrong, or you did not agree to be added, write to handcricket.ihcc@gmail.com with your registration number. A change to a name or to a WhatsApp number is made by IHCC.

Agreement

The board representative agreed to these terms before he added players. This page may be updated, and the date at the bottom shows when it was last changed.
