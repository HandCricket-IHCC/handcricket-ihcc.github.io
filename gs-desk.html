Joining requests

Complaints

Constitution 25.2: acknowledge in writing within twenty-four hours and tell the accused. The accused has three days to answer. Then the investigating officer has fourteen days.

Change a player's details

Rules 2.32 to 2.36. The player asks you in any way. When you are satisfied of his identity, find him and make the change. The old and the new value are saved in the log.

Squads below twelve

Rule Book 2.24: the board has thirty days from the notice. If the squad is still below twelve, you give a last period of fourteen days. The periods stop from the closing of the Voting List until the result of the election.

Meetings ordered by the Chairman

Constitution 13.8: call the meeting of the Directors with twenty-four hours' notice and write the notice in the Main Group. Then press the button.

Requests from the boards

Rules 2.27 and 8.9A. A move into the first twelve must be decided within twenty-four hours. A player who left is removed and his badge name goes into the reserve. To reject, write the reason first.
