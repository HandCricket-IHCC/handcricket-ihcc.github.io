Accessibility

How the IHCC website supports screen readers, keyboards and phones, and how to report a problem.

Our aim

The IHCC website should work for everyone, including people who use a screen reader, a keyboard, a phone with a large text size, or a dark screen. Nobody should need help from another person to read an announcement, find a board page or take part in an election.

What is already in place

  • A link "Skip to the content" at the top of every page.
  • One main heading on each page, with headings in order, so you can move by heading.
  • The menu is a real list, and the page you are on is marked as the current page.
  • Every picture that carries meaning has a text description. Decorations are hidden from screen readers.
  • Messages such as "Loading", "Closed" or errors are written in words, never only with a colour.
  • Light and dark screens follow your phone or computer setting. Pages can be zoomed. The menu buttons and links are at least 44 pixels high.
  • No pop-ups that open by themselves, no moving carousels, no sounds that start by themselves, no cookies and no third-party scripts.
  • Keys to move around without a mouse are listed on the keyboard shortcuts page.
  • Text and background colours were measured on the public pages.

What is not perfect yet

  • The site is only in English. Some words, such as the names of the Boards, are in the original spelling.
  • The pages were checked with automated tests of the page structure and the colours. Checking with several screen readers on several phones is still going on.
  • Some pages ask you to sign in with a Google account. The Google sign-in page is not made by IHCC.

Tell us about a problem

If something does not work with your screen reader, keyboard or phone, write to handcricket.ihcc@gmail.com with the subject "Accessibility problem". Please say which page, which phone or computer, and which screen reader you use. We will answer and fix it as soon as we can.
