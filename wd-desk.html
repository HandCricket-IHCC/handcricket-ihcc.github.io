Matters of women players

Constitution 14.15: you receive, together with the General Secretary, complaints about harassment and the safety of women players. Keep here only a short summary, with no names that must stay private. The decision stays with the Core Committee. You cannot give orders to a Member Board.

Participation of women players

Constitution 14.15(c): follow the number of women players in each board, using the register of the General Secretary, and encourage boards to bring women players.

Report and recommendations

Constitution 14.15(d) and (e): recommend tournaments or events for women players, and give the Board of Directors a report every three months. The Directors can read it.
